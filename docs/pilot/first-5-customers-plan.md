# First 5 Customers Plan

Goal: get 5 real pilot customers before public launch.

## Best customer types

- Coaching centres
- Clinics
- Small schools
- Local e-commerce websites
- Digital marketing agencies
- Small SaaS/startups
- Website developers with MSME clients

Avoid initially:

- Banks
- Government systems
- Large enterprises
- Websites without permission
- High-risk systems without proper agreement

## Pilot pricing

- Customer 1: Free, feedback required
- Customer 2: Free, testimonial required
- Customer 3: ₹499 pilot
- Customer 4: ₹999 pilot
- Customer 5: ₹999 pilot or agency referral

## Daily outreach plan

Day 1:

- Make list of 20 local businesses.
- Find website and WhatsApp/email.
- Send 10 outreach messages.

Day 2:

- Follow up with 10.
- Send 10 new messages.
- Book first demo.

Day 3:

- Onboard first customer.
- Get authorization.
- Run safe review.
- Prepare report.

Day 4:

- Deliver first report.
- Ask for feedback.
- Onboard second customer.

Day 5:

- Improve report language.
- Ask for testimonial.
- Start paid pilot offer.

## Simple pitch

Hi, I am running a private pilot for SecureMSME AI. It checks basic website security readiness for small businesses and gives a simple report with fix steps. It is authorized, safe, and non-destructive. I am selecting 5 pilot websites this week. Would you like me to review your website?

## Success metrics

Minimum:

- 5 authorized websites
- 5 reports delivered
- 3 useful feedback responses
- 1 testimonial
- 1 paid customer or serious paid intent

Strong:

- 5 reports delivered
- 3 testimonials
- 2 paid customers
- 1 agency wants to use it for clients
