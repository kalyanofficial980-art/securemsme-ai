# Manual Billing SOP

This SOP is for first private pilot customers.

## Pilot pricing

Recommended:

- First 2 customers: free in exchange for feedback/testimonial.
- Customer 3: ₹499 pilot.
- Customer 4: ₹999 pilot.
- Customer 5: ₹999 pilot or agency referral.

## Payment rule

Do not auto-activate paid access from payment screenshots or fake success links.

Every payment requires manual human review.

## Accepted payment process

1. Customer asks for pilot.
2. Confirm authorization and scope.
3. Share price and deliverables.
4. Customer pays manually.
5. Verify payment manually.
6. Record payment status.
7. Create or update customer workspace.
8. Run safe scan/report workflow.
9. Send report after human review.

## Do not collect sensitive payment data

Do not ask for:

- Card number
- CVV
- UPI PIN
- Netbanking password
- OTP
- Screenshots containing sensitive bank details

Allowed:

- Payment date
- Amount paid
- UTR/reference ID
- Sender name
- Plan selected
- Customer email

Use: Your payment will be manually reviewed before activation.

Do not use instant activation, admin access, guaranteed compliance, or complete security guarantee language.
